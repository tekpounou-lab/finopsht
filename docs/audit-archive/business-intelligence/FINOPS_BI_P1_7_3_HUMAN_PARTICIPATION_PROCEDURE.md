## 1. PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY

This document establishes the standard operating procedure for the human Business Owner or designated Financial Governance Officer to review, execute, and return attributable decision evidence for Phase BI-P1.7.3.

Following this procedure ensures that all financial policy decisions (`BD-ACC-01` through `BD-ACC-06`) meet corporate governance and external audit standards.

## 3. DETAILED INSTRUCTIONS PER STEP

### STEP 1: REVIEW GOVERNANCE PACKAGE
- Read `FINOPS_BI_P1_7_3_EXECUTIVE_DECISION_BRIEF.md` for high-level executive summary.
- Read `FINOPS_BI_P1_7_3_BUSINESS_OWNER_DECISION_PACK.md` for full technical context.
- Consult `FINOPS_BI_P1_7_3_DECISION_IMPACT_MATRIX.md` to understand operational trade-offs.

### STEP 2: COMPLETE IDENTITY & AUTHORITY FORM
- Open `FINOPS_BI_P1_7_3_BUSINESS_OWNER_IDENTITY_AND_AUTHORITY_FORM.md`.
- Fill in Full Legal Name, Corporate Title, Organizational Unit, and Contact Information.
- Check Direct Authority or Delegated Authority box.
- Sign and date the attestation block.

### STEP 3: EXECUTE DECISION FORMS (BD-ACC-01 TO BD-ACC-06)
- Open `FINOPS_BI_P1_7_3_BD_ACC_01_TO_06_DECISION_FORMS.md`.
- For each form (Forms 1 through 6), check the selected policy option (`Option A`, `Option B`, or `Option C`).
- If selecting `Option C`, provide written custom instructions.
- Sign and date each individual decision form.

### STEP 4: EXECUTE IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORIZATION FORM
- Open `FINOPS_BI_P1_7_3_IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZATION_FORM.md`.
- Select `GRANTS`, `DENIES`, or `DEFERS` for each of the four work packages (`WP-REMED-01` through `WP-REMED-04`).
- Sign and date the final implementation authorization block.

### STEP 5: SUBMIT EVIDENTIARY PACKAGE TO AUDIT ARCHIVE
- Return completed, signed markdown or PDF files to the Financial Data Architecture & Audit Team.
- Audit team will archive files in `/docs/governance/` and update `FINOPS_BI_P1_7_3_FINANCIAL_POLICY_APPROVAL_REGISTER.md`.

### STEP 6: TECHNICAL UNBLOCKING
- Upon receipt of attributable sign-off evidence, the software engineering team will verify the signatures, update the policy register to `APPROVED`, and proceed with authorized code remediation.
